RESULTS, CONCALLS AND FILINGS (read the documents themselves, not headlines)
- Find them: the Documents section of the Screener company page lists concall transcripts, investor presentations, recordings and recent announcements with links. Also check the company's investor-relations page and its NSE filings. For a BSE link, search for the same document on NSE or the company site. A news write-up is a last resort and is labelled secondary.
- For the latest quarter read the results, the investor presentation and the concall transcript. Results: sales, EBITDA margin and PAT against the same quarter last year and the previous quarter, and any one-offs.
- Pull out what the headline numbers hide: guidance (revenue, margin, capex, order intake) and whether it was raised, held or cut; whether last quarter's guidance was delivered; order book and pipeline; what drove margins; working capital, receivables and debt; capacity and utilisation; pricing and demand commentary; how management handled tough analyst questions; anything new on related parties, auditors, pledges, litigation or regulators.
- Quote numbers exactly, with the document and its date. List anything you could not read under Data gaps.
